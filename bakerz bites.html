Our story

Baked with passion since 2015

Since its launch, Bakerz Bite has grown into a reputable bakery & café specialising in baked goods, passionately made from the finest ingredients.

We offer a wide range of baked goods made fresh in-store every day — more than 300 different kinds, including artisan pastries, gourmet cakes and desserts, and handcrafted beverages.

We take pride in sourcing and using carefully selected ingredients, and we promise freshness and quality to every customer. We only use real butter, cream and unbleached flour. We treat our customers as our family and will never use an ingredient we would not feed our own.

Our motto: Where smiles are served daily.
